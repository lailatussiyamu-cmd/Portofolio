Data audit

ICU Antibiogram × Guideline Audit

Cross-checked the hospital's empiric antibiotic guideline against ~2,400 clinical isolates from four intensive-care units. Mapped dominant pathogens and resistance patterns per unit and specimen. Only two of eight covered conditions were fully supported by local data — surfaced MRSA at 39–55% and carbapenem-resistant A. baumannii at 79–100%.

Data analysis

Berlin Real Estate Market Analysis

Cleaned and analyzed Berlin property listing data across price, location, and rental yield. Grunewald came out as the priciest district at €11,121/m², while Mitte posted the steepest growth at 45% since 2020. Neukölln offered the best rental yield (a 23-year price-to-rent payback), and prices in the highest-priced areas ran roughly 3× those in the lowest — with the sharpest jump landing in 2022, the post-pandemic year, and no strong correlation found between listing volume and price.

Python pandas Data visualization
