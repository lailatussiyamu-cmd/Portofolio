ICU Antibiogram × Guideline Audit

Local resistance data vs. empiric antibiotic policy

Cross-checked the hospital's empiric antibiotic guideline against ~2,400 clinical isolates from four intensive-care units. Mapped dominant pathogens and resistance patterns per unit and specimen, then scored all eight covered clinical conditions: only two were fully supported by local data. The audit surfaced two coverage gaps the guideline had no answer for — MRSA at 39–55% and carbapenem-resistant A. baumannii at 79–100% — and closed with six prioritized revision recommendations, delivered as both a slide deck and an interactive web report.

Data analysis HTML Chart.js PowerPoint Clinical microbiology

SynClinicsLIVE

Web referral platform for fast-track ICU & PICU admissions

A synchronized referral network connecting outside hospitals to intensive-care beds. Referring sites submit a patient and required documents through one web form; the receiving unit sees the case before the ambulance arrives, which cuts the paperwork and phone relay that used to eat into the golden window. Built from a root-cause analysis of referral delays and evaluated after rollout: 94.2% patient satisfaction and 95% satisfaction among referring hospitals.

Web platform Process design Root-cause analysis Service evaluation

PediDripLIVE

Pediatric continuous-infusion calculator · 14 drugs

Weight-based syringe-pump rate calculator for PICU continuous infusions — sedation, vasoactives, and neuromuscular blockers across five dosing-unit systems. Designed around patient safety: no data persists between patients, color appears only when it carries clinical meaning, and every screen assumes a two-nurse double check.

FOOTSAFE-90

Foot drop & pressure injury prevention programme

A quality-improvement programme across ICU and PICU, built on a problem that moves quietly: immobilised patients lose ankle function and skin integrity over days nobody is counting. Eligibility screening runs off Excel dashboards, progress is tracked in interactive Chart.js visualisations, and the whole cycle is documented in a PDCA framework with implementation reporting.
